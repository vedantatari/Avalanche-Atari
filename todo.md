# Avalanche: fun and retention to-do

Ranked by fun gained per unit of effort. Suggested order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5, with the quick wins slotted in between.

## Main items

- [ ] **1. Make combos feel big** (small effort)
  - The game already counts catch streaks and pays combo bonuses, but players mostly see the streak afterwards ("best combo" on the result card).
  - Show a live combo counter near the cart that grows with each catch.
  - Raise the catch sound's pitch as the streak climbs.
  - Add a burst at 10, 20 and 30 in a row.
  - Losing the streak should sting a little (a short "streak broken" cue).

- [ ] **2. Make carts matter** (medium effort)
  - The 10 carts are looks only right now. Give each one a small perk, for example:
    - slightly wider scoop
    - faster movement
    - weak built-in magnet
    - frost doesn't slow you
    - one fire rock per level is blocked
  - Keep perks small so every level stays fair on every cart.
  - Unlocking a cart should feel like a reward, and choosing one should be a decision.

- [ ] **3. Daily missions that pay shields** (small–medium effort)
  - Three small goals each day, for example:
    - catch 5 emeralds
    - clear a level without losing a heart
    - reach a 15 combo
  - Reward: shields. They already power the restore button, so no new currency is needed.
  - Purpose: a reason to open the game again tomorrow.

- [ ] **4. Boss level every 10th level** (medium effort)
  - Levels 10, 20, 30 and so on become set pieces. They are the same levels where a new cart unlocks.
  - Ideas:
    - a 20-second gold avalanche where the whole cliff collapses
    - a giant demon rock that chases the cart
  - Breaks up 100 similar levels and gives each chapter an ending.

- [ ] **5. Leaderboard** (medium effort, already planned)
  - Ask for the player's name once per device.
  - Leaderboard button showing the top 5 players by highest level and best score.
  - Backend: Supabase.
  - Seeing someone one spot above you drives replays better than almost anything.

## Quick wins

- [ ] **Final rush:** double points in the last 5 seconds (the music already changes for the final 10).
- [ ] **Near-miss moment:** when a fire or demon rock barely misses the cart, flash "Close!" with a tiny slow-motion.
- [ ] **Trophy rewards:** give each trophy a small prize (shields or a cart colour) so they're worth chasing.

## Not planned

- More rock types. There are already about 15, and more mostly adds confusion.
- Extra difficulty for its own sake.
